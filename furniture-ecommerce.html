Forma Living

Furniture discovery, customization & guided checkout

A mobile furniture shopping experience that brings product discovery, material selection, saved favorites, and checkout into one consistent interface.

The Challenge

Furniture shopping involves more than choosing a product. Shoppers need to compare finishes, understand price differences, and review delivery details before placing an order. The design challenge is to make those decisions manageable on a small screen.

The Solution

The interface gives the product image room to lead, then groups material choices, color swatches, quantity, and price around the purchase decision. A staged checkout separates address, shipping, payment, and order review.

The Shopping Experience

Discover the collection

A visual catalog, category navigation, search, and filters provide entry points for exploring furniture.

Choose the details

The product screen places fabric options and color swatches beside a clear price and add-to-cart action. The selected material can display a price adjustment.

Keep choices organized

Favorites preserve items for later, while the cart shows quantities, individual products, and a summary of costs.

Review before ordering

Four checkout stages lead to an overview and an order confirmation screen with delivery information.

Design Takeaways

The visual hierarchy keeps product imagery prominent while giving configuration and checkout their own clear spaces. Mint, lavender, and dark navy connect the browsing and purchase screens.
