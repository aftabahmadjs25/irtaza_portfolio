Overview

Taj Support is a mobile customer support and team communication app built for Taj Technology Star1. It empowers support and sales teams to handle live customer inquiries from their phones — complete with department routing, voice notes, document & media attachments, and a searchable customer directory.

Crafted with a crisp, distraction-free interface, agents can triage pending conversations, claim or transfer chats across departments, and resolve issues faster without being tied to a desktop desk.

Key Capabilities

Live Queue & Triage

Department Filtering: Instantly switch between All Departments, Sales, and Support queues.
Smart Status Tabs: Filter conversations by All, Pending, or Assigned to Me with live unread counts.
Session Lifecycle Events: Clear inline system markers for chat assignment, agent joins/leaves, and inactivity timeouts.

Rich Messaging & Customers

Voice Notes: Record and play back crisp audio messages directly inside support threads.
Multi-Format Attachments: Share documents, camera captures, gallery photos, and audio clips seamlessly.
Customer Directory & Profiles: Quick access to customer records and agent profile controls.
