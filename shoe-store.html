SoleStep

Sneaker discovery, size selection & order tracking

A sneaker shopping app interface that connects a visual catalog with product options, a shopping bag, account details, and a delivery timeline.

The Challenge

Sneaker shoppers need to move from inspiration to a specific pair, color, and size. After purchase, they need an understandable view of where their order stands. The design challenge is to keep those choices and status details easy to scan.

The Solution

Image-led product cards lead into a detail screen with color and size choices. Search suggestions support discovery, the bag groups purchase details, and the account area provides a route into order history and tracking.

The Shopping Experience

Browse and search

Category chips, product cards, recent searches, and popular search terms help shoppers explore the catalog.

Select a pair

The detail view groups color choices, sizes, sizing-system labels, price, and an add-to-bag action around the shoe image.

Review the bag

The cart presents the selected item, quantity controls, a promo-code field, shipping, and the total before checkout.

Follow an order

The tracking view presents an estimated delivery window, tracking number, carrier, package contents, and a timeline from order placement to delivery.

Design Takeaways

Orange highlights connect selections, purchase actions, and the current delivery milestone. The order timeline distinguishes completed, current, and pending steps, extending the shopping journey beyond the bag.
